## Purpose

This folder contains Architecture Decision Records and other approved decision
records. It is the historical record of why a decision exists.

## Required decision types

- Architecture decision
- Protocol decision
- Governance exception
- Scope or phase decision
- Technology or dependency decision
- Reversal or deprecation decision

## Boundary rule

Decision records explain decisions. They do not replace the canonical policy,
architecture contract, or specification that implements the decision.
